Privacy Policy

AI Room Makeover Planner

Effective date: November 8, 2026

Bright Apps provides AI Room Makeover Planner, an application that helps users explore room design ideas using artificial intelligence. This policy explains how information is processed when you use the application.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, we process:

  • Room photos you select and AI-generated designs.
  • Room preferences, design instructions, notes, and budget information.
  • Account identifiers associated with Apple or Google sign-in.
  • App installation identifiers and authentication information used to protect accounts and credits.
  • In-app purchase transaction records, credit balances, and related verification information.
  • Technical information needed for app operation, security, and troubleshooting.

2. Artificial Intelligence Processing

When you request an AI room design or planning result, relevant room images and design instructions are transmitted through our backend to Google Gemini, a third-party AI service operated by Google.

This information may include uploaded photos, generated designs, room type, preferred style, budget, and notes. Avoid including sensitive personal information in your photos or instructions.

The application will request your consent before sending your information to the AI service. If you decline, the request will not be submitted.

3. Storage of Images and Projects

Projects and associated images may be stored locally on your device. Certain generated images and related metadata may also be stored on Google Cloud to support result recovery and service operation.

Recovery availability does not necessarily represent the period for which backend copies are retained. Images you separately save to your device's photo library are managed through your device.

4. Accounts and Purchases

Apple and Google sign-in services may be used to authenticate your account. In-app purchases on iOS are processed through Apple.

We maintain purchase verification records, transaction identifiers, credit balances, and related financial records to prevent duplicate credit grants, protect purchases, and resolve transaction issues. We do not receive your complete payment card number from Apple.

5. Service Providers

We use Apple, Google sign-in services, Google Gemini, and Google Cloud where applicable to provide authentication, purchases, AI generation, storage, and backend functionality.

Information is shared with these providers as necessary for the relevant functionality. Their processing may also be subject to their respective privacy policies.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained according to its operational, security, and legal purpose. Some purchase and financial records may need to be retained after account deletion for transaction reconciliation, fraud prevention, dispute handling, or applicable legal obligations.

For questions about specific records and retention, contact us using the email below.

7. Account Deletion

You can request account deletion through the application settings. Account deletion includes removal of account identity associations and processing of associated personal-data cleanup.

Certain financial records may be retained where necessary for legitimate transaction and legal purposes. Photos saved separately to your device's photo library are not removed by deleting your app account.

If you have unused purchased credits or an unresolved transaction, contact support before deleting your account so we can explain the available options.

8. Your Choices

You may choose which photos to upload, decline AI processing consent, delete locally stored projects using available app controls, and request account deletion. You may contact us with privacy questions or requests concerning your information.

9. Security

We use authentication, access controls, and other technical safeguards designed to protect application information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Updates

We may update this policy when our services or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

11. Contact
