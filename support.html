AI Room Makeover Planner — Support

We're here to help with AI image generation, project access, in-app purchases, render credits, and account questions.

Contact support

Email: brightapps.support@gmail.com

When contacting us, include the app name, a brief description of the issue, your device model, and the approximate time it happened. Screenshots are helpful when safe to share.

For your security: Never send passwords, authentication codes, full payment card numbers, or sensitive room photos unless necessary and you are comfortable sharing them.

AI render did not complete

Check your internet connection and try again. If the app reports an error or a render credit appears to have been used without a successful result, contact support with the approximate time and what the app displayed.

Missing purchased credits

First, reopen the app and allow time for purchase verification or recovery. If your balance remains incorrect, email support with the package purchased, approximate purchase date, and a redacted App Store receipt or transaction reference if available. Do not send payment card details.

Refunds

For purchases made through Apple's App Store, you can request a refund using Apple's Report a Problem website. You may also contact us for help understanding a purchase or credit balance.

Account deletion and privacy

To request deletion of an account, use the Delete Account option in the application's settings. If deletion fails, you have unused purchased credits, or you need assistance with personal data, contact support. Some financial records may be retained where needed for transaction reconciliation or legal obligations; see our Privacy Policy.

AI processing

AI Room Makeover Planner uses Google Gemini to process room photos and design instructions for requested AI features. Review the Privacy Policy before submitting images or instructions.
